**[EFTofLSS_nu](https://github.com/roger2b/EFTofLSS_nu)** — Mathematica notebooks for the tree-level bispectrum of large-scale structure with massive neutrinos, from [de Belsunce & Senatore (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.06849).

---

**[likelihood-approximations](https://github.com/roger2b/likelihood-approximations)** — Python code that approximates a likelihood from the moments of the data, developed for the large-angle Planck polarization analysis in [de Belsunce et al. (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14378).

---

**[Bayesian-parametric-fitting](https://github.com/roger2b/Bayesian-parametric-fitting)** — Fast Bayesian extraction of a cosmological signal from multi-frequency maps, used to test for spectral-index variations in polarized foregrounds in [de Belsunce et al. (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13968).

---

**[smooth_covmat](https://github.com/roger2b/smooth_covmat)** — Fortran code that builds smooth covariance matrices from the Planck hit-count maps.

---

**[MAPLE](https://github.com/bhorowitz/MAPLE)** — Maximum a posteriori estimator for the 3D Lyman-α forest power spectrum and its covariance, written in JAX with Benjamin Horowitz and Zarija Lukić ([Horowitz, de Belsunce & Lukić 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17294)).
